# Restraint, taste and track record

**Date:** 2026-11-24
**Source:** dictated, project chat
**Topics:** topics/restraint-taste-and-discernment.md, topics/ai-jagged-displacement-in-product-making.md

Going to the other side of the spectrum, we can talk about how it's not just about going more quality on everything. Sometimes the differentiation is actually the limitation. And this is where we start understanding what mastery taste and these things are actually about. It's just about, it's just as much about what you choose to do as what you choose not to do. South Park is not funny, despite it being lo-fi, cutout, fast animation. It is funny because of that. They understood that going lo-fi actually made the humor more funny. often quality and differentiation is a byproduct of restraint . That restraint is something that a lot of people identify as taste. The ability to explore a limitation, to its logical conclusion, is a powerful differentiator. just because you can add more production value doesn't mean you end up with a better product. We often like things that understand and have the wisdom to strip back to the essentials. We can feel when there is discernment in a product. Many products succeeded because they understood something that people would want if they just tried it, and then they taught people how to do it, and then people learned that they, in fact, did like it. When you create that behavioral change, that creates incredible trust. Because now people view you as someone who actually understands what to make and what people will like once it's made. We cannot shortcut this. You need a track record. People who are simply prompting AI will not be able to get that track record . They may get lucky a couple times where the limitations or the scope of what they were building happens to map very nicely to something that users like, but the question is whether they have enough discernment to really think about things and deliver that high quality repeatedly.
