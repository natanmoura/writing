So the key point here is that what matters to different products or ideas and what people will care about is not some kind of evenly distributed thing. It is actually jagged. Some products will not benefit from a better 3D model. Some products will live and die on whether they can deliver on those. In the same way that some products will live and die by the game feel of the kickback of a gun, and others will not.
